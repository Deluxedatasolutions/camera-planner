Background

Satellite is worldwide imagery. QLD aerial is the Queensland Government photography, usually sharper in regional towns. Own image lets you work off any screenshot or photo.

Click to choose an image, drag one here, or just paste (Ctrl+V) a screenshot from Google Maps, Nearmap or QLD Globe.

To set the scale, click Set scale, then click two points a known distance apart (the Google Maps scale bar, a 6 m wide driveway, a 2.4 m fence panel) and type the distance.
